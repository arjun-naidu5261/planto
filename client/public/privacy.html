Privacy Policy for PlantMe

Last updated: October 13, 2026

Welcome to PlantMe ("we," "our," or "us"). We are committed to protecting your personal information and your right to privacy. This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, and share information when you use our mobile application and services.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account Information: Name, phone number, email address when you create an account.
  • Delivery Address & Location: Delivery location to show nearby nurseries and deliver plants to your address.
  • Order Details: Items purchased, order history, and payment status (payments are processed securely via certified payment gateways).

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To process, fulfill, and deliver your orders from verified local nurseries.
  • To provide live order delivery GPS telemetry updates.
  • To provide plant care guides and plant passport recommendations tailored to your space.
  • To communicate customer support updates and notifications.

3. Data Protection and Security

We do not sell your personal data. We implement industry-standard encryption and security measures to protect your personal details.

4. Your Rights and Choices

You can request to view, update, or delete your account data at any time by contacting us at support@plantme.app or through the in-app profile settings.
