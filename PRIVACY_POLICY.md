# Privacy Policy for PlantMe

**Last updated:** October 13, 2026

Welcome to **PlantMe** ("we", "our", or "us"). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how our mobile application and services collect, use, and safeguard your data in compliance with Google Play Developer Policies.

---

### 1. Information We Collect

When you use the PlantMe app, we may collect the following types of information:

- **Personal Information:** Name, email address (`arjunuddagiri@gmail.com`), and phone number provided when creating an account or signing in.
- **Delivery Address & Location Data:** Physical address and approximate or precise GPS coordinates provided to locate verified nearby nurseries, calculate delivery distance, and deliver live plants.
- **Order & Transaction Details:** Items purchased, order notes, plant care preferences, and transaction IDs. Payment card details are securely handled by certified third-party payment gateways; we never store your credit card or bank passwords.
- **Device & App Performance Data:** Crash logs, app diagnostics, and device model to ensure stable app performance.

---

### 2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information solely for the following purposes:
- To display nearby nursery plant inventories and facilitate plant orders.
- To provide real-time order delivery tracking and driver GPS updates.
- To personalize plant passports and plant care schedules based on your local sunlight and climate.
- To process customer support requests and app updates.

---

### 3. Data Sharing & Third-Party Services

We **do not sell, rent, or monetize** your personal data. We only share necessary data with trusted service providers:
- **Verified Local Nurseries:** To fulfill plant orders and prepare plant packages.
- **Delivery Logistics Partners:** To deliver your plants to your designated address.
- **Google Play Services:** For app distribution, performance monitoring, and crash reporting.

---

### 4. Data Retention & Deletion

We retain your personal data only as long as necessary to provide services and fulfill legal obligations. You may request deletion of your account and associated personal data at any time by contacting us at **arjunuddagiri@gmail.com** or through your in-app profile.

---

### 5. Children's Privacy

PlantMe is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

---

### 6. Security

We implement industry-standard encryption (HTTPS/TLS) and strict access controls to protect your data from unauthorized access or disclosure.
